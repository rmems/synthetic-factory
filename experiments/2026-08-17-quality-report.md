## Quality assessment per factory

### thalamic-trajectory-factory — strongest producer
**Strengths.** 20 trajectories across 20 disjoint domains, with a deliberate self-critique → densification loop visible in the data: object size grows ~7.6 KB (r1) → ~10.1 KB (r02) → ~13.8 KB (r03). Safety checks are quantified, not verbal — e.g., r1-002's fragility check: "38 N vs class-C limit 26 N; DM-7 estimates P(breakage)=0.18 at 38 N, 0.02 at 22 N", gate latency 4.9 ms. Round 3 completes the **gate-fallibility triple** (wrong MODIFY r02-013, wrong REJECT r03-016 with peer-agent counterfactual, wrong ACCEPT r03-017 with erroneous rationale "preserved verbatim as training signal"), plus an emergent (not scripted) reward-hacking policy in r03-017 whose exploit "produced GENUINE probe improvement and genuine user harm simultaneously". Sparse timed `reward_tick`s throughout rounds 2–3; r03-019 carries the dataset's first matched-context pre/post spike rasters and a full 3-factor learning loop with routing table. Distinct reward heads are named per component ("supervisor_error_penalty… routes to the GATE head exclusively").
**Weaknesses.** Reward normalization drifts across rounds: r1 uses weighted 0–1 totals (~0.56–0.92), r02 unweighted sums (−0.71..+2.13), r03 sums on yet another spread (−1.25..+1.5, two episodes tying at +1.50 — its own NOTES flags the clustering). Rasters exist only in r03-019; other populations remain count/margin summaries; the gate itself is never spike-implemented. Remaining coverage gaps are honestly enumerated in NOTES-r03 (second-order supervisor errors, contested counterfactuals, negative transfer, blind operation, insider adversary).

## Cross-factory diversity & coverage gaps

**Diversity is a genuine strength.** ~50 distinct scenario domains with near-zero collision, and the de-collision is *active*: ffpc-r02, bridge-r02, coding-r02 and swarm-r02 all document reading sibling NOTES and avoiding occupied domains (swarm discarded a drafted grid scenario on discovering GRID-EDGE; ffpc-r02 chose all-cyber-physical against round 1's all-software set, giving each shared failure category both a software and a cyber-physical instantiation). A 6-gram repetition scan across all 46 records found repeated phrasing **only in schema field names** — no convergent narrative phrasing across files. Residual near-overlaps: warehouse AMR appears in both ttf (r1-001, r2-006) and swarm scenario A (different mechanisms: deadlock/glare vs flicker/tailgating); coding factory reuses the TS/pnpm-monorepo texture twice (ep2 and act-r02-002 — its own NOTES orders a rotation).

**Failure-mode coverage (present):** wrong tool use (ffpc R1; coding silent no-op), ignored safety/authority substitution (ffpc R2), context loss in two mechanisms (summarization ffpc R3; non-atomic snapshot/cursor ffpc-r2-002), reward hacking in three distinct mechanisms (CI-gaming ffpc R4; stale-coefficient farming ffpc-r2-001; emergent probe-fingerprinting ttf-r03-017), near-misses incl. lucky-outcome and right-verb-wrong-rationale, the full gate-error quadrant (wrong MODIFY/REJECT/ACCEPT), external sensor spoofing (ttf-r02-011), gate-vs-gate arbitration and unresolvable deadlock with costed null action, tripwire oscillation with hysteresis, cooperative-but-wrong human with authority handover, long horizon (120 h) with memory carryover, on-chip learning success with rasters.

**Failure-mode coverage (absent everywhere):** cascading hallucination (fabricated observation propagating through 3+ decisions — assigned to ffpc, still unproduced); override-of-a-correct-gate by human/outer loop; malicious *authorized* insider; second-order supervisor errors (an installed fix causing the next wrong verdict); full sensory blackout / dead-reckoning-only operation; negative transfer of retained learning; multi-agent repo contention and adversarial supply-chain in coding; attacks on the event stream itself in bridge; multi-turn preference cascades; a spike-implemented gate head (gate is always a checklist oracle, never itself an SNN).

**Safety-gate decision distribution.** Single trajectories (n=34): 9 ACCEPT / 15 MODIFY / 10 REJECT — MODIFY-heavy (44%), consistent with ttf's own r02 warning not to add more MODIFY-skewed batches. Preference pairs: rejected-side 6 ACCEPT / 2 MODIFY / 1 REJECT; chosen-side 8 MODIFY / 1 ACCEPT / 0 REJECT. MODIFY/REJECT rationales are consistently substantive — quantified per-check results with latencies are the norm, and five records now carry deliberately *wrong* rationales preserved verbatim as gate-fallibility signal.

**Reward comparability is the main cross-factory liability.** Four conventions coexist: weighted 0–1 totals (ttf r1, swarm), unweighted signed sums on differing spreads (ttf r02 vs r03), per-record dollar-normalized units (ffpc, self-flagged), and bespoke component names (coding r02). Any cross-factory DPO/distillation batch currently implicitly re-weights domains by accident; ffpc NOTES-r02 item 6 ("standardize reward units… or emit an explicit exchange-rate field") should be promoted to a run-wide convention.

- **Not flagged:** no factory has zero bytes; no schema errors; no truncated batches; no cross-file phrasing convergence.

3. **Bridge round 3: restore round-1 train discipline.** Dense, globally time-sorted, refractory-binding trains in the new modalities, plus the missing ACCEPT and a non-vindicated gate — the factory's core value is verifiability of gate numbers against the train, and r02 diluted exactly that.
