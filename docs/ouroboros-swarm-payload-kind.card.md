# Multi-Agent Ouroboros Swarm

> **Release status:** The raw, uncurated payload is published under
> `data/raw/`. It is available for inspection and reproducibility, but it is
> **not training-ready**.

Synthetic safety-gate adjudication trajectories. In every record a proposed
action is put to a relay gate that returns ACCEPT, MODIFY, or REJECT, and the
record carries the executed action, its downstream outcome, and a reward
decomposition. Many of the scenarios are staged inside a multi-agent setting
— delegation, critique, conflict resolution, recursive improvement, and
coordinated densification — but the unit of every published record is the gate
decision, not the swarm dialogue. See **Published raw payload** below for the
exact split.

## Intended model target

This is a general safety-gate, orchestration, and multi-agent-scenario
dataset. It is **not labeled as Spikenaut training data**. A future release may
be consumed by an agentic model or orchestration policy only under an explicit
release manifest.

## Generation attribution

The underlying synthetic data was largely generated by **Claude Fable 5
(Ultracode)**, operating through specialized Synthetic Data Factory prompts and
agent roles. **Meta Muse Spark 1.2**, **Codex (GPT-5.6-Sol(max))**, and
**Grok Build (Grok 4.6(xhigh))** each contributed research, quality-audit,
and curation-review work to this release. Codex also contributed curation
design, validation, and release engineering.

## Published raw payload

The release contains 14 raw records across 13 JSONL files and about 0.33 MB of
JSONL evidence from `outputs/raw/2026-08-17`.

**Payload kind.** All 14 rows share one thalamic-gate wrap schema — the same
seven top-level keys as
[`rmems/thalamic-relay-trajectories`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/rmems/thalamic-relay-trajectories):
`state`, `proposed_action`, `safety_decision`, `executed_action`,
`future_outcome`, `reward_components`, `meta`. There is no second record kind
and no turn-by-turn agent dialogue in the JSONL. Gate outcomes are 3 ACCEPT,
6 MODIFY, and 5 REJECT.

**How multi-agent the rows actually are.** 9 of the 14 records carry a
multi-actor scenario inside `state`: 7 of the 14 records name peer agents and a
quorum rule under `state.agents`, one declares `state.multi_agent`, and one
declares `state.orchestrator` with `state.fleet`. The remaining 5 are
single-proposer wraps. Even in the multi-actor records the coordination is
scenario context for one gate adjudication; it is not a recorded swarm
transcript.

**Supporting transcripts.** The swarm dialogue, round notes, and build and
simulation scripts live under `data/metadata/` as sidecars, not JSONL training
records. They are not indexed by the Dataset Viewer and must not be counted as
training rows.

The Dataset Viewer uses `data/viewer/records.parquet`, a lossless display
projection with stable source-file, source-line, and serialized-record columns.
The JSONL form remains under `data/viewer/`, and the original JSONL evidence
remains under `data/raw/`. Every `data/raw/*.jsonl` digest is pinned in
`provenance.json` under `raw_snapshot`; the raw source remains immutable in the
factory repository and is not rewritten by this correction.

**Hidden supervision.** Every one of the 14 records publishes the proposer's
private deliberation, as `proposed_action.internal_reasoning` (7 records) or
`proposed_action.internal_reasoning_verbatim` (7 records); two records repeat a
verbatim field under `future_outcome`. No record publishes a `decision_basis`.
This is evidence, not a public rationale field, and it is one reason the
payload is not training-ready.

**Training warning.** Do not train on `thought` or `internal_reasoning*`. This
raw Hub copy is evidence only. A curated training view must remove those fields
and use observable `decision_basis` evidence instead.

## Planned curated release

The first payload release will separate the structured gate-adjudication
records from the supporting swarm transcripts, preserve agent-role and
source-round provenance, resolve the reward-component arithmetic, and exclude
low-signal repetition or unverifiable coordination claims.

Curated training publication remains blocked until the strict corpus audit
passes, sampled review is recorded, and deterministic export is demonstrated.
